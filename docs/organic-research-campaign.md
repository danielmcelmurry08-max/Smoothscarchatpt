## Evidence boundaries
Use the linked publications for original summaries; do not republish journal photos or full PDFs as ad assets. The BJD trial studied SPARC-targeting siRNA, not every BioRNA SKU. Avoid claims of proven gene silencing for hydrolyzed RNA, complete one-hour absorption, guaranteed depth or rapid results. The 2021 study concerned preclinical glaucoma surgery; the 2022 study used cells and mice; the 2024 dermatology article is commentary. Study-patch length is not guaranteed penetration depth. Keep context next to trial figures. No result guarantee, C-section-specific efficacy claim or U.S. clearance claim is established by these sources.
