## Testimonial-gathering plan

Invite genuine customers after they have used the product and genuine providers after they have evaluated or offered it. Ask for honest feedback, including difficulties. Do not require positive feedback, write their answers or make a discount depend on praise. Disclose any incentive or commercial relationship when publishing.

Customer questions:
1. Which product did you use, and for how long?
2. What made you consider it?
3. How did applying and wearing it fit your routine?
4. What was easy, and what was difficult?
5. What would you want someone considering it to know?
6. Is there anything you would change?
7. May we contact you to confirm the wording and your publishing preferences?

Provider questions:
1. What is your role, and which product did you evaluate or offer?
2. How did you explain the routine to patients?
3. What questions did patients ask?
4. How did it fit your practice workflow?
5. What support or information would improve that process?
6. What would you tell another practice considering it?
7. May we confirm your name, credentials, practice attribution and any commercial relationship before publication?

Consent wording:
“Smooth Scar Inc. would like to publish your feedback about [product] on [specific website pages and/or named channels]. Participation is optional and does not affect your care, purchase or relationship with us. We will send you the exact quote and attribution for approval before publication. We may shorten it for length without changing its meaning, subject to your approval. Please choose whether we may use your name, first name only, or no name. Photos, video and health details require separate permission. To ask us to stop future use or remove material from channels we control, email helixbiodyne@rayesolutions.com. We cannot guarantee removal of copies already shared by others.”

Separate unchecked approvals: exact quote/channels; chosen attribution; separately specified photograph/video; separately specified health information. Record consent date, approved version and any incentive. Seek practice authorization before using a practice name or logo. Do not solicit unnecessary medical history. Review any outcome statement against the approved claim list before publishing; consent alone does not substantiate a claim.
