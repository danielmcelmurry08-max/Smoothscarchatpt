Evidence with context

Promising results. Clear limits.

Here are the approved BioRNA study and survey findings. They describe groups and individual responses, not a promise of what your scar will look like.

Reduce scar volume by up to 95%

From a small study of 50 patients over 60 days, measured with a high-resolution 3D scanner. “Up to” is the highest reported reduction, not an average or a guaranteed result.

88% report better healing than silicone gel sheet

A reported experience measure. It does not mean that every person will have the same result.

8 out of 10 prefer BioRNA Antiscar over silicone gel sheets

A product preference finding. Preference is different from a measured change in scar size.

85% reported no worsening or elevation after stopping use

At 16.1 months. This finding does not guarantee that a scar will stay unchanged.

These findings should not be read as separate BioRNA-C, C-section or orthopedic study results. We do not claim that every product or scar type will have the same outcome.

Use only on fully closed skin. Do not use on open or infected wounds. Follow the supplied instructions and ask your care team if you are unsure when to start.

What is in the patch?

Dissolving hyaluronic acid micro-tips and hydrolyzed RNA. The patches hydrate and protect. Follow the supplied instructions for application and wear.

Keep research and product results separate.

Published research on a SPARC-targeting siRNA patch is different from the commercial hydrolyzed-RNA product. We do not use that research patch’s results as BioRNA product results.

A question for your care team.

Is my wound fully closed and ready for scar care? BioRNA-C is positioned for C-section scar care, with a twice-weekly overnight routine. That positioning does not mean the findings above establish C-section-specific results.

Why we do not show a Day 1 / Day 3 result.

The owner-supplied photographs show one 16-year-old scar at different angles and in different lighting. They do not establish a treatment effect or predict results. We have removed them from the sales comparison.
