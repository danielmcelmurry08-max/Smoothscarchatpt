Usage protocol

BioRNA is marketed for cosmetic, physician-recommended scar-appearance care after procedures in plastic surgery, obstetrics, dermatology, and scar revision. Start only after the wound is fully closed, including after stitch removal; never use on open, infected or broken skin.

BioRNA Daily (Antiscar)

One fresh patch nightly, 8 hours overnight. Ask the care team how long to continue. Seven single-use patches per box.

BioRNA-C

One fresh patch twice weekly, 8–12 hours overnight. Ask the care team how long to continue. Eight single-use patches per box.

Micro-tip section: 1 × 6 cm. For longer scars, follow the manufacturer’s guide for trimming the adhesive edge only and placing patches side by side. Do not cut the micro-tip section. Steroid-free; external use only.

Coverage can require multiple patches. Follow the enclosed product-specific instructions and clinical assessment. Published head-to-head research compared an experimental SPARC-targeting siRNA patch with silicone sheeting in the same patient; equivalence to the marketed hydrolyzed-RNA formulations is not established.

Clinical research your team can review.

A 2024 randomized study compared a SPARC-targeting siRNA microneedle patch with silicone sheets in postoperative scars. It did not directly establish equivalent effects for every marketed hydrolyzed-RNA formulation, for BioRNA-C, or for orthopedic procedures.

The study used daily 12-hour application after wound union. Product schedules and clinical claims require product-specific documentation. Request the available materials before making patient recommendations.
