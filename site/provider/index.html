Easy to explain. Simple to use.

Patients place a fresh patch on fully closed skin and wear it overnight, following the supplied instructions. Dissolving hyaluronic acid micro-tips and hydrolyzed RNA offer an at-home scar-care routine without mixing or measuring.

BioRNA Daily (Antiscar)

7 single-use 1 × 6 cm patches per box. A fresh patch each night, worn for 8 hours overnight.

BioRNA-C

8 single-use patches per box. Positioned for C-section scar care, used twice weekly for 8–12 hours overnight, such as Monday and Friday.

Clinical research your team can review.

A 2024 randomized study compared a SPARC-targeting siRNA microneedle patch with silicone sheets in postoperative scars. It did not directly establish equivalent effects for every marketed hydrolyzed-RNA formulation, for BioRNA-C, or for orthopedic procedures.

The study used daily 12-hour application after wound union. Product schedules and clinical claims require product-specific documentation. Request the available materials before making patient recommendations.
