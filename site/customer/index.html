Your step-by-step routine

How to use your patch

Start only after the wound is fully closed. Do not use on open, infected or broken skin, even if stitches have been removed. Follow your supplied product instructions and ask your care team if unsure.

BioRNA Daily (Antiscar) — every night, 8 hours
  1. Cleanse and dry — Start only after the wound is fully closed, including after any stitch removal. Skin must be clean and fully dry. Never use on an open or infected wound.
  2. Apply before bed — Place the micro-tip section over the scar and hold gentle, steady pressure for 30 seconds.
  3. Wear overnight — Leave on for 8 hours. Follow the instructions supplied with your selected product.
  4. Replace daily — One new patch each night for a suggested 6–8 weeks, following your care team’s advice and the supplied product instructions. For a longer scar, trim only the adhesive edge as directed and place patches side by side. Do not cut through the micro-tip section.

The suggested 6–8-week routine is not a guaranteed time to a result. Coverage may require more than one patch per application.

BioRNA-C — twice weekly, 8–12 hours
  1. Cleanse and dry — Start only after the wound is fully closed, including after any stitch removal. Skin must be clean and fully dry. Never use on an open or infected wound.
  2. Apply before bed — Place the micro-tip section over the scar and hold gentle, steady pressure for 30 seconds.
  3. Wear overnight — Leave on for 8–12 hours. Follow the instructions supplied with your selected product.
  4. Replace twice weekly — One new patch twice a week for a suggested 6–8 weeks, following your care team’s advice and the supplied product instructions. For a longer scar, trim only the adhesive edge as directed and place patches side by side. Do not cut through the micro-tip section.

The suggested 6–8-week routine is not a guaranteed time to a result. Coverage may require more than one patch per application.

Research with context

Understand the evidence.

Published human research compared a specific SPARC-targeting siRNA microneedle patch with silicone sheets for postoperative scars. This does not establish equivalent outcomes for every commercial hydrolyzed-RNA product or for BioRNA-C specifically. Results vary.

The published study used a different application protocol; always follow your product’s supplied directions and use only after the wound has fully closed.

Your questions, answered

A clearer next step.

What should I do after wearing a patch?

Remove the patch after its scheduled wear time and discard it. Use a fresh patch for each application. If a rash develops, stop use and contact your clinician. For a missed application or questions about creams under the patch, check the enclosed instructions or ask your care team before changing the routine.
