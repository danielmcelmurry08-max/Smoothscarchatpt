How it works

Apply. Wear overnight. Get on with your day.

Each patch uses dissolving hyaluronic acid micro-tips and hydrolyzed RNA. The patch hydrates and protects, with no mixing or measuring.

  1. Check your skin. Begin only when the wound is fully closed. Never use on an open or infected wound.
  2. Apply a fresh patch. Follow the instructions supplied with your product.
  3. Follow your schedule. Daily: 8 hours each night. BioRNA-C: 8–12 hours overnight, twice weekly.

Research with context

Understand the evidence.

Published human research compared a specific SPARC-targeting siRNA microneedle patch with silicone sheets for postoperative scars. This does not establish equivalent outcomes for every commercial hydrolyzed-RNA product or for BioRNA-C specifically. Results vary.

The published study used a different application protocol; always follow your product’s supplied directions and use only after the wound has fully closed.

When can I start?

Only after the wound is fully closed. Do not use on open or infected wounds. Ask your care team if you are unsure when to begin.

Which patch should I choose?

BioRNA Daily (Antiscar) is used each night for 8 hours. BioRNA-C is positioned for C-section scar care and used twice weekly for 8–12 hours overnight, such as Monday and Friday.

What comes in a box?

BioRNA Daily (Antiscar) has 7 single-use 1 × 6 cm patches. BioRNA-C has 8 single-use patches. Use a fresh patch for each application. The amount you need depends on scar coverage.
