How to use

  1. Cleanse and dry — Start only after the wound is fully closed, including after any stitch removal. Skin must be clean and fully dry. Never use on an open or infected wound.
  2. Apply before bed — Place the micro-tip section over the scar and hold gentle, steady pressure for 30 seconds.
  3. Wear overnight — Leave on for 8–12 hours.
  4. Replace twice a week — One new patch on Monday and Friday, following your care team’s advice and the supplied product instructions. Ask your care team how long to continue. For a longer scar, trim only the adhesive edge as directed and place patches side by side. Do not cut through the micro-tip section.

Coverage needs may require more than one patch per application.

Questions about BioRNA AntiScar

Which patch should I choose?

BioRNA AntiScar is used twice weekly for 8–12 hours overnight, such as Monday and Friday, for post-surgical scars. BioRNA-C uses the same twice-weekly routine and is positioned for C-section scar care.

When can I start?

Only after the wound is fully closed. Do not use on open or infected wounds. Ask your care team if you are unsure when to begin.

What should I do after wearing a patch?

Remove the patch after its scheduled wear time and discard it. Use a fresh patch for each application. If a rash develops, stop use and contact your clinician. For a missed application, check the enclosed instructions or ask your care team before changing the routine.
