7 single-use patches per box · 1 × 6 cm per patch

Manufacturer's routine: a fresh patch each day, worn overnight for 8 hours. Ask your care team how long to continue.

  1. Begin only after the wound is closed and the scab has come away. Clean and dry the skin.
  2. Open the pouch and protective case. Lift the patch by a corner without handling the central micro-tips.
  3. Peel away the backing, position over the scar, and hold steady pressure for 30 seconds.
  4. Remove the patch after the recommended overnight wear period. Discard it and use a fresh patch for the next application.

Do not apply to open or infected wounds. Follow the supplied product instructions.
