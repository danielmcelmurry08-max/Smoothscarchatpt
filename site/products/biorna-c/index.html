Standard box · 8 single-use patches

Manufacturer's routine: apply on two spaced days each week, such as Monday and Friday, with 8–12 hours of overnight wear. Ask your care team how long to continue.

  1. Start with clean, dry, fully closed skin. Open a fresh patch without touching its micro-tips.
  2. Remove the adhesive liner and position the micro-tip surface against the scar.
  3. For coverage with two patches, follow the illustrated manufacturer guide for trimming the adhesive edge and placing the patches together.
  4. Hold gentle pressure for 30 seconds. After the overnight wear period, discard the patches. Each application requires fresh patches.
Can I use BioRNA-C for an orthopedic scar?

BioRNA-C is positioned by the manufacturer for C-section scar care. Ask your care team for a product-specific assessment before using it for an orthopedic scar. Do not use on open or infected wounds. Patch quantity depends on scar coverage.
