Clinical review checklist

Specificity builds confidence.

What was studied?

SPARC-targeting siRNA in dissolving hyaluronic acid microneedles, compared with silicone sheets on opposite halves of a postoperative scar.

What does the mechanism tell us?

SPARC-targeting gene-silencing research provides a mechanistic rationale. It does not establish that hydrolyzed RNA or a different commercial formulation produces the same biological effect.

What remains to be established?

Product equivalence, U.S. intended use, and evidence specific to TKA/THA scars, pigmentation, mobility, and keloid prevention require separate verification.

01 · Daily application

BioRNA Antiscar
Daily box

7 single-use patches per box · 1 × 6 cm per patch

Manufacturer's routine: a fresh patch each day, worn overnight for 8 hours. The suggested course is 6–8 weeks; confirm the duration with your clinician.

  1. Begin only after the wound is closed and the scab has come away. Clean and dry the skin.
  2. Open the pouch and protective case. Lift the patch by a corner without handling the central micro-tips.
  3. Peel away the backing, position over the scar, and hold steady pressure for 30 seconds.
  4. Remove after the recommended overnight wear period. Discard it and use a fresh patch for the next application.

Do not apply to open or infected wounds. Follow the supplied product instructions.

02 · Twice-weekly application

BioRNA-C
2 times per week

Standard box · 8 single-use patches

Manufacturer's routine: apply on two spaced days each week, such as Monday and Friday, with 8–12 hours of overnight wear. Suggested duration: 6–8 weeks, adjusted with your clinician.

  1. Start with clean, dry, fully closed skin. Open a fresh patch without touching its micro-tips.
  2. Remove the adhesive liner and position the micro-tip surface against the scar.
  3. For coverage using two patches, follow the illustrated manufacturer guide for trimming the adhesive edge and placing the patches together.
  4. Hold gentle pressure for 30 seconds. After the overnight wear period, discard the patches. Each application requires fresh patches.

Manufacturer positioning: C-section scar care. Orthopedic use needs a separate product-specific clinical assessment. Do not use on open or infected wounds. Patch quantity per application depends on scar coverage.
