Your questions, answered

A few clear answers to help you find your next step.

What is Smooth Scar?

We bring BioRNA scar-care patches to customers in the United States. Smooth Scar Inc. is its own standalone company and the home of the BioRNA patch.

What is in a BioRNA patch?

The patch has tiny dissolving tips made with hyaluronic acid and hydrolyzed RNA. The tips melt into the skin during overnight wear. There is nothing to mix or measure.

Which routine should I explore?

BioRNA Daily (Antiscar) uses a fresh patch every night. BioRNA-C uses a twice-weekly routine for C-section scars. Follow the instructions supplied with your product. Explore both patches.

When can I start?

Start only after the wound is fully closed. Do not use the patches on open or infected wounds. If you are unsure whether your skin is ready, ask your care team before starting.

Can I reuse a patch?

No. Use a fresh, single-use patch for each application and discard it after wear. Follow the supplied product instructions.

What do the testimonials tell me?

The six stories come from the BioRNA product carousel, “Hear from our users.” We keep the words and names as supplied. They describe personal experiences, not study results or a promise of what you will see. None of these six stories is a C-section story. Read the user stories.

What does the clinical research actually show?

A 2024 trial evaluated a specific SPARC-targeting siRNA microneedle patch against silicone sheets, not necessarily every marketed hydrolyzed-RNA product. Its findings do not guarantee results for BioRNA Daily (Antiscar) or BioRNA-C. Read the study and limitations.

Where can I see comparison pictures?

Our Results page shows real, day-labeled examples with context. The owner-supplied foot photos appear there only, labeled as one person’s experience, not a study result. Photos can differ in angle, lighting and scale, and do not predict your result.

How long will it take to see a change?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Scars and individual responses vary. The days shown in photos describe those examples only. Ask your care team how long to continue your routine.
