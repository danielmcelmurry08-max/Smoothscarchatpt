Case stories

Elbow hypertrophic scar (published case, Ann Acad Med Singap 2024;53:113-6).

Pain 8/10 at the start. Pain gone after 2 days. Flatter and softer after 5 days. Better still after 22 days.

This published case used an experimental siSPARC nanoplex-loaded HA patch, not an established retail BioRNA product result. The authors also described symptoms returning and scar-volume increase after use stopped. Read Chai et al.

Surgery, injections, and silicone sheets had all been tried. After a year of on-and-off use: itching mostly gone, top keloid visibly flatter, keloids softer to the touch, no pain.
