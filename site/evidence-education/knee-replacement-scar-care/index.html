Scar care after knee replacement

When can I use a scar patch after knee replacement?

Follow your orthopedic team’s incision-care instructions. Before starting BioRNA AntiScar, ask your team to confirm that the incision is fully closed and ready for a scar-care patch. Never apply it to an open or infected wound.

At follow-up, ask whether any dressing or closure strips should stay in place and whether your scar is longer than the patch. The product page lists patch dimensions so you can discuss coverage before ordering.

How do I use BioRNA AntiScar once my incision is ready?

BioRNA AntiScar uses a fresh patch twice a week, worn overnight for 8 to 12 hours. Ask your orthopedic team how the routine fits your recovery plan.

See BioRNA AntiScar patch sizes, instructions and pricing

What if I’m considering silicone sheets?

Bring your surgeon the names of the products you are considering and ask which routine suits your scar. Our comparison of BioRNA and silicone sheets explains how the options differ.
