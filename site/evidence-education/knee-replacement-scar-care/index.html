Scar care after knee replacement

When can I use a scar patch after knee replacement?

Follow your orthopedic team’s incision-care instructions. Before starting BioRNA AntiScar, ask your team to confirm that the incision is fully closed and ready for a scar-care patch. Never apply it to an open or infected wound.

At follow-up, ask whether any dressing or closure strips should stay in place and whether your scar is longer than the patch. The product page lists patch dimensions so you can discuss coverage before ordering.

How do I use BioRNA AntiScar once my incision is ready?

BioRNA AntiScar uses a fresh patch twice a week, worn overnight for 8–12 hours. Ask your orthopedic team how the routine fits your recovery plan.

Your questions, answered

Questions about scar care after knee replacement

When can I start BioRNA AntiScar after knee replacement?

Only after your orthopedic team confirms the incision is fully closed and ready for a scar-care patch. Keep following your team's incision-care instructions, and never apply the patch to an open or infected wound.

How often do I use BioRNA AntiScar?

Use a fresh patch twice a week, on spaced days such as Monday and Friday, and wear it for 8–12 hours overnight. Use a new patch for every application — each patch is single use. Ask your orthopedic team how long to continue and how the routine fits your recovery plan.

What is BioRNA AntiScar made of?

BioRNA AntiScar is a patch of dissolvable hyaluronic acid micro-tips with hydrolyzed RNA. The micro-tips dissolve into the skin overnight and deliver the RNA where the scar is forming. It is not a silicone sheet or gel.

What if my scar is longer than one patch?

Each patch measures 1 × 6 cm. For a longer scar, trim only the adhesive edge, as directed in the supplied instructions, and place patches side by side along the scar. Do not cut through the micro-tip section. If you are unsure about coverage, ask your orthopedic team before ordering.

What does the evidence show?

In a small manufacturer-reported study, scars treated with the BioRNA patch were down by up to 95% in volume — up to is the highest reported result, not the average. In the same study, scar volume was 30% lower than with silicone sheets. Individual results vary. See the full evidence, with sources and limits.

What if my incision becomes red, warm, or sore?

Contact your orthopedic team promptly. Increasing redness, warmth, pain, or discharge can be signs of infection, and your team should check the incision before you use any scar-care product on it.
