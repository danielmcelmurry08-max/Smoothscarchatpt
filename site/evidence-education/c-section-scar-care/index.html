C-section scar care

When can I start using a patch on my C-section scar?

Start BioRNA-C only after the entire incision is fully closed. Ask your maternity team to check the incision if you are unsure. Never apply the patch to an open or infected wound.

If you notice increasing redness, pain, or discharge, contact your maternity team promptly. Continue following your discharge instructions for incision care.

How do I use BioRNA-C once my incision is ready?

The BioRNA-C routine uses a fresh patch twice weekly on spaced nights, such as Monday and Friday, for 8–12 hours overnight. Check the supplied instructions and ask your OB-GYN how long to continue.

What if I’m considering silicone sheets?

Bring your surgeon the names of the products you are considering and ask which routine suits your scar. Our comparison of BioRNA and silicone sheets explains how the options differ.
